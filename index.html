Research

Published · Sept 2026
IJEDR · Vol. 14, Issue 3 · Paper ID IJEDR2603479

Confidential Computing: A Secure Hardware-Anchored Foundation for AI and Cloud Computing

Data is usually encrypted at rest and in transit, but sits unprotected while it is being processed. The paper compares hardware-isolated trusted execution environments (Intel SGX/TDX, AMD SEV-SNP, ARM CCA and NVIDIA confidential GPUs), walks through remote attestation, weighs the performance cost for AI workloads, and covers side-channel attacks and post-quantum attestation.

With Riya Prakash and Dr. L. C. Manikandan
